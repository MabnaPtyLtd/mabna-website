Carpark Upgrade, North Kellyville

❮ ❯

Client

Australian International Academy

Location

North Kellyville, NSW

Sector

Civil Infrastructure

Delivery

Construct Only

Project Summary

Mabna was engaged to coordinate and deliver carpark upgrade works for Australian International Academy in Kellyville. The project involved civil construction within an active school environment, requiring careful planning, staging and communication to minimise disruption to the school community.

The works included design coordination, bulk earthworks, stormwater infrastructure, kerb works, electrical coordination and pavement construction. The project required a practical and well-managed approach to sequencing, safety and access management.

Project Scope

  • Design coordination and construction planning.
  • Bulk earthworks and subgrade preparation.
  • Kerb and concrete works.
  • Stormwater drainage installation.
  • Electrical infrastructure coordination.
  • Pavement construction and carpark finishing works.

Key Challenges

  • Delivering works within an operating school environment.
  • Managing access, safety and staging around school operations.
  • Coordinating multiple trades and services within a compact work area.
  • Maintaining program while managing civil, drainage and electrical interfaces.

Key Success Factors

  • Detailed planning before commencing site works.
  • Safe separation of construction activities from school operations.
  • Clear sequencing of earthworks, drainage, kerb and pavement works.
  • Responsive coordination between Mabna, the client and subcontractors.

Services Delivered

Mabna delivered design coordination and civil construction services for the carpark upgrade works, including bulk earthworks, subgrade preparation, stormwater drainage, kerb and concrete works, electrical infrastructure coordination, pavement construction and final carpark finishing works. The works were planned and sequenced to support safe delivery within an operating school environment while maintaining access and minimising disruption to school operations.
