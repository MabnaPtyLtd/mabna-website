Project Summary

Mabna was engaged to complete civil and landscaping works as part of a school upgrade at One School Global in Oatlands. The project required multiple work fronts to be delivered concurrently under a tight construction program.

The works included bulk earthworks, stormwater infrastructure, FRP OSD tank and footing works, lime stabilisation and play area installation. The project required close coordination of labour, plant, subcontractors and materials to maintain progress across several activities at the same time.

Project Scope

  • Bulk earthworks and site preparation.
  • Stormwater drainage works.
  • FRP OSD tank construction.
  • FRP footing works.
  • Lime stabilisation.
  • Play area preparation and installation works.
  • Landscaping and associated civil completion works.

Key Challenges

  • Managing several work fronts concurrently.
  • Maintaining progress under a tight deadline.
  • Coordinating civil, structural, drainage and landscaping interfaces.
  • Working within a school environment with heightened safety and access considerations.

Key Success Factors

  • Strong daily coordination between site crews and subcontractors.
  • Efficient staging of works to keep multiple activities progressing.
  • Practical sequencing of earthworks, drainage, FRP and play area works.
  • Focused site management to maintain safety, quality and program outcomes.

Services Delivered

Mabna delivered civil construction and landscaping support services for the One School Global upgrade works, including bulk earthworks, stormwater drainage, FRP OSD tank construction, FRP footing works, lime stabilisation, play area preparation and associated civil completion works. The works were coordinated across multiple concurrent work fronts to maintain program, safety and quality outcomes within the school environment.
